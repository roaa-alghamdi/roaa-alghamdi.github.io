01 / About

Business first, then the data

My degree sits inside the Faculty of Business Administration, so most of my coursework starts with a business problem: a telecom company losing complaint context between departments, a retailer deciding whether AI forecasting is worth the cost, an oil major whose margins shrank in a year.

That is how I like to work. I talk to the people involved, define the KPIs, clean and model the data, and build a dashboard that makes the next decision obvious. I use SWOT, PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces and VRIO when the question is strategic, and Excel Solver or decision trees when it is quantitative.

I also hold a professional diploma in cybersecurity, which taught me to think about who should see which data, and how to design access to it properly.

Nakheel Market: Retail Sales Dashboard

A three-page Power BI report for a fictional Saudi supermarket chain with 12 branches in 9 cities. I designed the dataset myself so I could practise on realistic, shareable data.

Graduation project · STC case study · 2026–27 · team of 4

Customer Context Continuity in Complaint Handling

Why do STC customers repeat their complaint every time it changes hands? We ran a survey of 250 people and 5 stakeholder interviews, defined 6 continuity KPIs, and built three Power BI dashboards: complaint journey, KPI view and an AI decision-support screen.

73.8% of complainants lost context at least once; the worst point is the hand-off between departments (52.9%). I led the problem definition, research questions and theoretical framework.

Saudi Aramco: Financial Performance & Ratio Analysis

Common-size statements and ratio analysis of Aramco's 2022 and 2023 consolidated results: liquidity, solvency, asset efficiency and profitability, with the figures modelled in Excel and summarised in Power BI.

Profitability fell with oil prices (net margin 30.1% to 27.5%), while liquidity stayed strong and reliance on debt kept falling.

MIS 1404 · case-based team project

ASTRA Markets: Should We Automate Replenishment?

A case-based study for a grocery chain weighing AI forecasting. I worked through break-even analysis, a linear program for the daily restocking mix, sensitivity analysis, a nonlinear profit model and a decision tree with imperfect information, all in Excel Solver.

Recommendation: buy the SAR 3,000 data study first; roll out AI only if the result is favourable, otherwise pilot at two stores.

Applied Security Project · team of 5

Target 2013 Breach: Analysis and Data-Security Redesign

How one phished HVAC vendor led to 40 million stolen cards. We mapped the kill chain, then redesigned access, data and network controls: RBAC with ABAC conditions, least-privilege SQL roles with row-level security, tokenisation and a segmented cardholder-data zone.

Tested on PostgreSQL 16, with a Python demo of SQL injection and the parameterised-query fix.
